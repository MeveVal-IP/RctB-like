Distribution of ER size and GC-content deviation among complete Vibrionales genomes.

Vibrionales n(ERs)=1777


Categories:
Left: linear y-axis. Right: log10 y-axis.
Each point represents one ER plotted according to its size relative to the host chromosome (%Chr) and its GC-content difference from the host chromosome (ΔGC = GCChr − GCER). ERs carrying canonical Chr2 RctB, divergent RctB-like or no RctB-like protein are shown in different colors. n(ERs) indicates the number of ERs from complete Vibrionales genomes. In this interactive version, hovering over a point displays the corresponding replicon information (GC content and Replicon size), category buttons can be clicked to show or hide groups, and the toolbar allows zooming, panning, resetting the view, and exporting the plot.
