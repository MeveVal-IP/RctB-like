Distribution of ER size and GC-content deviation among complete Vibrionales genomes.

Vibrionales n(ERs)=1172


Categories:
Left: linear y-axis. Right: log10 y-axis.
Each point represents one ER plotted according to its size relative to the host chromosome (%Chr) and its GC-content difference from the host chromosome (ΔGC = GCChr − GCER). ERs carrying canonical RctB, divergent RctB-like or no RctB-like protein are shown in different colors. n(ERs) indicates the number of ERs from complete Vibrionales genomes. Marginal distributions are shown as kernel-density violin plots, illustrating the distributions of ΔGC values (top) and ER size (%Chr; right). For the right marginal distribution, KDEs were calculated after log10 transformation of ER size. KDEs are normalized to the same maximum width, allowing comparison of distribution shapes but not absolute ER numbers. In this interactive version, hovering over a point displays the corresponding replicon information (GC content and Replicon size), legend entries can be clicked to show or hide categories, and the toolbar allows zooming, panning, resetting the view, and exporting the plot.
